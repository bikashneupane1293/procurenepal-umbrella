Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 (Nepal Time)

ProcureNepal is operated by Cuidado Technology Pvt. Ltd., Nepal ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use procurenepal.com and app.procurenepal.com, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number (if given), password (stored only as a one-way hash), your role and the organization you belong to.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your name, email address, whether Google has verified that email, and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Business data you enter: company profiles, certificates, tenders, bids, projects, bank guarantees, staff lists and documents you upload or type in. This data belongs to your organization.
  • Usage records: sign-in times and basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser) used for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: sign you in, show your organization's data, and run features such as tender qualification checks and reports.
  • To send service emails: account activation, staff invitations, join requests and plan or billing notices. We do not send marketing email without your consent.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Who can see your data

Data you enter is visible only to members of your organization, according to the roles your organization's Owner sets, and to our support staff when needed to help you. We never sell your data. We use trusted service providers to run the service — cloud hosting and database providers, Brevo for sending email, and Google for sign-in — and they process data only on our instructions. Bid prices and tender data are kept separate for each organization.

e-GP credentials

ProcureNepal never asks for or stores your e-GP (bolpatra.gov.np) login, and never submits bids on your behalf.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your organization's account is active. If a plan expires, data is kept (not deleted) so it is available on renewal. You can ask us to delete your personal account or your organization's data by emailing us; we will do so unless the law requires us to keep it.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and each organization's data is separated on the server. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your password private and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, in line with Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018).

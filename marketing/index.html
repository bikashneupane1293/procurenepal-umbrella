Everything a contractor's office needs

One login, one workspace — switch modules on or off as your business needs them.

📋

Bolpatra eGP System

Track every tender and bid, read a bid document's own checklist automatically, manage qualification and JV partnerships, and keep a litigation-history and bidder-profile record ready for submission.

🏦

Bank Guarantee Management

Bid security, performance, advance and retention guarantees per bank with expiry alerts, release tracking, and limit usage — linked automatically to the bid or project they belong to.

🏗️

Project Management

From a won bid to a live project automatically: BOQ, schedule, progress reports, S-curve, daily site reporting and document management with expiry alerts.

📦

Procurement & Inventory

Requisition → RFQ → comparison → purchase order → goods receipt → stock, with vendor management and payables — one connected chain.

🚚

Vehicle & Fleet

Maintenance, tax and insurance renewals, trip and movement logs, and per-vehicle earning vs. expense — know what every vehicle actually costs you.

👥

HR & Operations

Employee records, payroll with SSF, custom roles and titles, and sign-in history for Owners and HR to see who's working when.
